Mine AQL

One Python file, zero dependencies. You give your wallet, you choose what you provide, and your earnings (AQL + USDT) build up every epoch. All paid work really serves the AI.

1. Choose what you provide

Compute (GPU)

Your machine runs the network's model (…) and serves users (paid in USDT + AQL), checks other miners, proposes and measures improvements (AQL). You need a local OpenAI-compatible model server: Ollama, vLLM or llama.cpp.

Storage

Your disk hosts encrypted network data. You are paid in AQL for every proof of storage, which cannot be produced without holding the data.

With Ollama: ollama pull … then leave Ollama running. Permissively licensed model (Apache 2.0): unrestricted commercial use.

2. Your wallet

The EVM address (MetaMask, Rabby, Ledger…) that will receive your earnings. The miner never needs your private key.
